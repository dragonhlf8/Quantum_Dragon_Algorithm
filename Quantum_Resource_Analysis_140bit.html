Key Insights

1. Shor variants dominate: All top-ranked methods are Shor or Regev-Shor hybrids. Grover is irrelevant at this scale.
2. Space is the bottleneck: Even with 20,000 physical qubits, only ~1,500 logical qubits are available. Methods needing >1,500 logical qubits fail.
3. Parallel runs are your friend: Ekera-Gärtner and Regev-style methods split work into d~sqrt(n) small runs. Each run is tiny; total time is manageable.
4. HECC Genus-2 is still open: No reversible Jacobian group-law circuits exist yet. Cannot estimate resources.
5. 140-bit is within reach: With 20,000 physical qubits and fault-tolerant error correction, a 140-bit ECDLP puzzle is solvable in hours to days using 2026-era algorithms.
